Cinzel and Alegreya Sans come from the official Google Fonts repository (https://github.com/google/fonts, `ofl/cinzel` and `ofl/alegreyasans`),
together with their `OFL.txt` license files (copied here as `OFL-Cinzel.txt` and `OFL-AlegreyaSans.txt`).

The `* SDF.asset` files next to them are TextMesh Pro font assets generated from these fonts (static atlases: ASCII, Latin-1 and common punctuation).
