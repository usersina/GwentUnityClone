# The Witcher 3 interface art

The notification emblems (`Notifications/`), the ability flares (`Effects/`), and the gem and row-score images in
`Assets/UI/Board` (GemRed, GemGrey, ScorePlayer, ScoreOpponent) are The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt's own Gwent interface art,
property of CD PROJEKT RED. They were taken as extracted by the gwent-classic remake
(https://github.com/asundr/gwent-classic, as kept in https://github.com/RandomPianist/gwent-classic-v3.1). The hero's
fire, the medic's green flames, the sparks and the smoke are drawn by the game itself. Like the card art, they are used here in a free,
non-commercial fan project and are not covered by this project's license.
