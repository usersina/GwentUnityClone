# The Witcher 3 interface art

The notification emblems (`Notifications/`), the ability flares (`Effects/`), the end of battle emblems (`End/`), and
the gem and row-score images in `Assets/UI/Board` (GemRed, GemGrey, ScorePlayer, ScoreOpponent) are The Witcher 3:
Wild Hunt's own Gwent interface art, property of CD PROJEKT RED. They were taken as extracted by the gwent-classic remake
(https://github.com/asundr/gwent-classic, as kept in https://github.com/RandomPianist/gwent-classic-v3.1). The hero's
fire, the medic's green flames, the sparks and the smoke are drawn by the game itself.

Skellige's (Blood and Wine) come from the same extraction: its card back (`Cards/Back/SK`), and the Berserker, Mardroeme,
Skellige Storm and Avenger glyphs, cut out of gwent-classic's ability discs and set in this game's own discs
(`Cards/DescriptionIcon`, `Cards/Ability`, `Cards/Special`). Like the card art, they are used here in a free,
non-commercial fan project and are not covered by this project's license.
